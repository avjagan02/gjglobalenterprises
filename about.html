Our Story

GJ Global Enterprises is a Vellore-based import & export company with over 3+ years of experience moving Indian agricultural goods, textiles and handicrafts to clients worldwide — and bringing quality raw materials, machinery and consumer goods back into India.

We work directly with verified Indian suppliers and global logistics partners so our clients get a single point of accountability from procurement to last-mile delivery.
